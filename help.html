BEFORE YOU ORDER

Need a hand?

Orders are confirmed with us on WhatsApp. Here is how the handoff works.

01

Choose your parts

Add the items and options you need to your cart. The cart is a shopping list and does not reserve stock.

02

Share your details

In the cart, enter your name, phone number, and delivery address to prepare the order message.

03

Confirm on WhatsApp

Review the message and send it to the shop. Your order is not submitted until you send that message.

DELIVERY & PAYMENT

Confirm the details with us.

We will confirm product availability, delivery options and charges, and payment details with you in the WhatsApp conversation.

This site does not take payment, calculate delivery charges, reserve stock, or create an order record.
